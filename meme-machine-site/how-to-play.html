How to play
Meme Machine.

Get your friends in a room, pick a game mode, and see who comes out on top. No lengthy setup or app install.

Open the game ↗

Start a match

  1. Create a room. One player opens the game and creates a room.
  2. Invite your friends. Share the room code or invite link. Each player joins on their own phone, tablet, or computer.
  3. Choose match length. The host picks 4, 6, or 8 rounds.
  4. Play the round. Pick a caption or a themed choice before the timer ends.
  5. Watch the score. Points are shown after each round. The final round is worth double; the highest score wins.

Caption Clash

Read the setup and choose one of five ready-made funny captions. Once everyone has picked, the captions appear for a vote. Choose the caption you think is funniest. You can’t vote for your own entry. If two people play, a bot caption is added so there is another option to vote on.

Caption scoring

  • Each vote for your caption: +20 points
  • Round-winning caption: +10 bonus points
  • Unanimous winner with at least two votes: +20 bonus points
  • Voting for a winning caption: +5 points
  • Final round: double points

Don’t Copy Me

Choose one option from the category. The machine has already picked a secret option. Match that secret and lose points; choose any other option and earn points. Your pick is revealed when the round ends.

Don’t Copy Me scoring

  • Match the machine’s secret pick: −10 points
  • Choose a different option: +20 points
  • Final round: double points

Good to know

  • Players are not eliminated; everyone can play the whole match.
  • Keep the game tab open while your match is in progress.
  • Room codes are temporary. Create a new room for your next match.
  • Use friendly names and keep feedback respectful.
