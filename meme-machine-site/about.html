THE STORY BEHIND THE GAME

Small rules.
Big group energy.

Meme Machine is an independent browser party game built to make it easy for friends to laugh together, wherever they happen to be.

Play with your friends ↗

What is Meme Machine?

Meme Machine is a real-time multiplayer game for 2–8 players. One person opens a room and shares its code. Everyone joins on their own phone or computer, chooses answers, votes, and watches the score update together. There is no app download and no player account.

Made for a shared laugh

The game is designed around quick, social decisions. In Caption Clash, players choose a funny response from five options and vote for the caption they like best. In Don’t Copy Me, players make a themed choice and score by avoiding the machine’s secret pick. The rounds are short so the conversation stays with the people in the room.

How to play

Choose a 4, 6, or 8-round match, invite friends with a room code, and play from separate devices. Points are awarded during each round, and the final round is worth double. See the complete quick-start guide and scoring rules.

Who makes it?

Meme Machine is an independent project by Charan Teja, maintained through its public source repository. The current live game is hosted at memeachine.vercel.app.

Send feedback

Players can leave an optional rating or comment after a match. For a bug report or a game idea, use the project issue page. Please do not post private information in a public issue.
